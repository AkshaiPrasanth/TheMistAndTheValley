COMMERCIAL B2B FRAMEWORK

TERMS & CONDITIONS

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Scope: Wholesale Export Transactions

1. Business Model & Single Counterparty Structure

The Mist & The Valley operates as a principal export trading and sourcing desk.

Single Counterparty Guarantee: The Mist & The Valley acts as the sole contracting counterparty for the Buyer. International buyers interact and contract exclusively with The Mist & The Valley. All commercial offers, invoices, quality parameters, export documentation, and logistics coordination are issued directly by us. Origin suppliers and producers communicate exclusively with The Mist & The Valley, ensuring streamlined single-point accountability and commercial privacy for both buyers and suppliers.

2. Products & Specifications

Product categories displayed on our website represent sourcing capabilities across our Indian supplier network. Product availability is subject to seasonal crop harvests, market supply conditions, regional availability, and export regulations. Specific grades and parameters are binding only when explicitly set forth in a formal written commercial offer or Purchase Order confirmation issued by The Mist & The Valley.

3. Commercial Quotations & Pricing

Website inquiries and preliminary discussions do not constitute binding commercial offers. Official written quotations provided by The Mist & The Valley specify unit prices, currency (USD/EUR), trade terms (Incoterms 2020), minimum order quantities (MOQ), and validity periods.

4. Payment Terms & International Banking

Standard B2B trade payment arrangements are established on a per-transaction basis (T/T Advance + Balance against Shipping Documents, or Irrevocable Confirmed Letter of Credit at sight).
